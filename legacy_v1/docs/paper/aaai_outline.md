# AAAI Outline — Trustworthy Hybrid Wildfire Response (Phase 15B.6)

Working title (pick one):
- *Trustworthy Hybrid Coordination for Wildfire Response: Infrastructure-Aware Control and Cross-Regional Transfer*
- *When Deep RL Fails Safely: A Hierarchical Heuristic+RL System for Petroleum-Critical Wildfire Defense*
- *Cross-Domain Generalization of Strategic Wildfire Coordination under Critical-Infrastructure Risk*

## Framing

This is a **trustworthy AI-systems / safety-critical coordination** paper, not a "our RL beats baselines"
paper. The central empirical result is an honest **negative result** for naïve deep RL, which motivates a
**hierarchical hybrid** design and an **infrastructure-aware** objective. No sentence claims "PPO solves
wildfire suppression."

## Contributions

1. A reproducible, leakage-free geospatial wildfire-**response** benchmark with **infrastructure-aware,
   risk-weighted** objectives (not just burned cells).
2. A **hierarchical hybrid** architecture (interpretable heuristic low level + strategic high level)
   motivated by a rigorous negative result for naïve deep RL.
3. A **failure analysis** of single-agent PPO (policy collapse; reward-dominance), with the controls that
   expose it (effective-method gate, seed-integrity guard, disjoint eval seeds).
4. A **symmetric, infrastructure-aware cross-region transfer** study between desert (petroleum) and forest
   (mountain) regimes, with strategic metrics (ISR/WEL/CPS/RAC) and cross-domain generalization (TRS/CDGG).
5. **Critical-infrastructure protection** as a first-class objective for autonomous wildfire response
   (catastrophe penalty + cascading detonation + strategic prioritization).

## Section structure

1. Introduction — wildfire response, petroleum-critical regions, why trustworthiness matters.
2. Related work — RL for wildfire; hierarchical control; trustworthy / safety-critical AI; domain transfer.
3. Problem formulation — risk-weighted MDP; state tensor; infrastructure (asset types, criticality, blast).
4. Hybrid architecture — heuristic low level (routing + suppression) under a strategic high level
   (sector dispatch + asset prioritization); why hierarchical decomposition is the safe design.
5. Negative result & failure analysis — PPO collapse to near-constant action; reward-dominance diagnosis;
   the controls that expose it.
6. Experiments — heuristic MARL scaling; symmetric infrastructure-aware transfer; strategic coordination
   (greedy_risk vs risk_aware); strategic ablations (§15B.8).
7. Trustworthy-evaluation protocol — reproducibility, gates, provenance, determinism, CI.
8. Limitations & broader impact.

## Provenance rule

Every quantitative sentence in `report.md` maps to a committed `results/*.csv` and (for trained artifacts)
a `results/runs/<run_id>/manifest.json`. Tables are generated by `scripts/build_report_tables.py` — no
hand-entered numbers. The report-consistency CI test (`tests/test_report_consistency.py`) blocks drift.
