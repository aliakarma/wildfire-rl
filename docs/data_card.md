## Cell2Fire landscapes (V2, Phase 2)

Generated by the deterministic converter `python -m wildfire_marl.data.to_cell2fire
--region {saudi,california} --grid 32` into `data/cell2fire/{Saudi,California}/`
(committed; hashed into `results/data_manifest.json`; byte-identical across runs).

**Fuel model — Canadian FBP, one shared mapping.** Cell2Fire's fire behavior is the Canadian
Forest Fire Behavior Prediction System, so fuels are FBP types. Physical NDVI is mapped to
FBP classes with fixed region-independent thresholds
(`wildfire_marl/data/fuel_mapping.py`, citations therein: Forestry Canada Fire Danger Group
1992 ST-X-3; Pais et al. 2021; Tucker 1979; USGS NDVI interpretation):
`<0.05→Non-fuel · 0.05–0.15→O-1a · 0.15–0.30→O-1b · 0.30–0.45→C-7 · 0.45–0.60→C-5 · ≥0.60→C-3`.
Thresholds are expert judgment over those sources and are a **declared sensitivity
parameter** (Phase-10 ablation). Resulting distributions: Saudi ≈ 87% O-1a + 12% NF
(desert grass regime); California ≈ 19% NF (incl. ocean), 21% grass, 60% conifer classes.

**Physical NDVI recovery (per region, documented inverses).**
- *Saudi:* V1 recorded `fuel=(NDVI+1)/2` → exact inverse `NDVI=2·fuel−1`; cross-checked
  against the raw MODIS tif (×10⁻⁴ scaling): Pearson r=0.87, MAE=0.0075, means 0.094 vs 0.0944.
- *California:* ⚠️ **data debt** — V1 used an unrecorded min-max (raw NDVI tif not archived),
  so NDVI is reconstructed with the declared assumption `NDVI ≈ −0.05 + 0.95·fuel`
  (`ASSUMED_CA_NDVI_RANGE`). Re-download of raw California MODIS NDVI is required to close
  this; until then it is covered by the Phase-10 fuel-map sensitivity ablation.

**Weather.** ERA5 June-2025 6-hourly ROI extracts → spatial-mean series → linear
interpolation to hourly → `Weather.csv`. TMP °C; RH from t2m/d2m (Magnus,
Alduchov & Eskridge 1996); WS km/h; **WD is the meteorological FROM-direction** (Cell2Fire
adds 180° internally, `ReadCSV.cpp` — convention verified by the sim-smoke drift check).
FWI codes (FFMC/DMC/DC/ISI/BUI/FWI) computed daily with the standard CFFDRS equations
(Van Wagner 1987; Van Wagner & Pickett 1985; start-up 85/6/15) over the **full month**, then
the final 144 hourly rows (June 24–30, the most fire-mature window) are emitted —
Cell2Fire holds weather in a fixed 150-slot buffer (`Cell2Fire.cpp:40`), which bounds
episodes at ~143 one-hour agent steps. Precipitation: the archived extracts carry no
precip variable; both ROIs are June-dry → APCP=0 (documented simplification).

**Declared abstractions (identical for both regions).**
- *Representative-tile cell size:* `Forest.asc` declares 100 m cells (Cell2Fire's validated
  operating resolution) while patterns come from ~17 km ROI cells; absolute geographic
  distance is not preserved. Real per-cell lat/lon are kept in `Data.csv` (used by FBP
  foliar-moisture only).
- *Topography (SRTM; closes the V1 flat-topography data debt):* V1 archived slope
  *magnitude* only (no DEM/aspect), so early Phase-2 landscapes set ps/saz/elev=0.
  `scripts/fetch_srtm_dem.py` now builds a 3-arcsec (~90 m) elevation mosaic per ROI from
  the AWS Terrain Tiles skadi/SRTM set (ocean bathymetry and voids clamped to sea level),
  and `Data.csv` carries real per-cell integers: `elev` = block-mean elevation (m); `ps` =
  block mean of the ~90 m slope percent — steepness at the declared 100 m cell size, not a
  near-zero ~17 km plane fit; `saz` = azimuth of the block-mean gradient vector, i.e. the
  dominant **uphill** direction (FBP convention — `fbp.c` pushes the slope-equivalent wind
  along saz like waz). `elevation.asc` is emitted alongside; the V1 `slope.asc` stays as a
  provenance cross-check (Pearson r against the new ps recorded in
  `conversion_report.json`). With `jd` unset, `elev` is inert in FBP foliar moisture, so
  the behaviourally active change is the ps/saz slope effect (strong in California's
  mountains, near-nil on Saudi's plains).
- *Time semantics:* one fire period = 1 simulated minute (`--Fire-Period-Length 1.0`);
  weather advances every 60 periods; `steps_per_action=60` ⇒ one agent action per simulated
  hour.

**Ignition protocol (leakage-free).** FIRMS detections → grid cells (north-up ROI
registration) → restricted to burnable cells (`ignition_candidates.json`; in Saudi this
excludes 509/2792 detections on non-fuel — Gulf-coast industrial flares). Sampling is
detection-count-weighted with **disjoint train/eval seed streams**
(`wildfire_marl/data/ignition.py`, `scenario_seed_offset=100000`, ported V1 protocol).
Default `Ignitions.csv` = most-detected burnable cell (deterministic).

## Summary

Wildfire-RL builds a 7-channel geospatial **state tensor** per region from four public
remote-sensing sources. Raw data is **not redistributed** in this repository; it is
downloaded from the providers (see the per-source notebooks under
`notebooks/Data Preliminary/`) and processed into tensors.

## Regions

| Region | Directory | ROI (lon/lat rectangle) | Regime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saudi Eastern Province | `saudi_eastern_province` | `[45.5, 23.5, 50.5, 28.5]` | desert |
| Northern California | `california` | `[-124.5, 36.5, -119.0, 41.5]` | forest/mountain |

Temporal coverage: **June 2025** (single month — a documented limitation for seasonality).
CRS: **EPSG:4326 (WGS84 geographic, lon/lat)**, north-up registration (row 0 = max latitude,
col 0 = min longitude) to the ROI rectangle.

## Channels & sources

| Channel | Source | Dataset | License / policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| fire | NASA FIRMS | active fire (VIIRS/MODIS) | NASA data use policy; requires FIRMS map key |
| fuel | MODIS | NDVI (e.g. MOD13Q1, 250 m) → fuel density | NASA LP DAAC data policy |
| wind_x, wind_y | ECMWF | ERA5 10 m u/v components | Copernicus license (attribution) |
| temperature | ECMWF | ERA5 2 m temperature | Copernicus license |
| humidity | ECMWF | ERA5 2 m dewpoint → humidity proxy | Copernicus license |
| terrain | NASA/USGS | SRTM DEM → slope | public domain (US Gov) |

> ⚠️ A known ERA5 quirk: the time coordinate is `valid_time` (not `time`). The preprocessing
> handles this; see the ERA5 notebooks under `notebooks/Data Preliminary/`.

## Processing

1. Download raw per source → `data/<region>/raw/...`.
2. Resample/clip to the ROI grid; min-max normalize **per channel** to [0, 1]
   (`wildfire_rl.data.normalize.minmax_normalize`).
3. Stack into `(7, G, G)` (`G ∈ {32, 64}`) → `data/<region>/grids/GxG/state_tensor.npy`
   with `state_metadata.json`. Frozen channel order: `fire, fuel, wind_x, wind_y, terrain,
   temperature, humidity` (`wildfire_rl.data.CHANNEL_ORDER`).

The Saudi region additionally carries a **petroleum-asset criticality raster** (Phase 15):
`data/saudi_eastern_province/grids/32x32/criticality.npy`, a single-channel `[0, 1]` map built by
`python -m wildfire_marl.infra.build_infrastructure` (Gaussian falloff around public
petroleum sites), used as an
asset-weighted reward term — it is **not** part of the frozen 7-channel state tensor.

## Normalization caveat (important for transfer)

Per-region min-max means a value of 0.5 in Saudi ≠ 0.5 in California in absolute terms.
For cross-region transfer, use a **shared scaler** (`data/normalize.fit_shared_minmax`) so
absolute climate/terrain differences are not normalized away.

## Intended use / limitations

- Intended for **research** on wildfire-suppression RL and domain transfer — not operational
  firefighting decisions.
- Single month, two regions, simplified fire physics (no combustion chemistry, ember
  transport, or atmospheric coupling). See the report in `docs/paper/`.

## Provenance & integrity

Guards and manifests (V2 modules, ported from V1 in Phase 0):

- `python -m wildfire_marl.reproducibility.validate_tensors` — per-channel shape/finite/`[0,1]`
  invariants against `wildfire_marl.data.CHANNEL_ORDER` (CI-blocking).
- `python -m wildfire_marl.reproducibility.make_manifest` —
  [`results/data_manifest.json`](../results/data_manifest.json): SHA-256 + byte size for every
  tensor, raster, CSV, `.asc` landscape file, and JSON record under `data/` (including the
  generated `data/cell2fire/` landscapes), plus `results/models_manifest.json` when
  checkpoints exist.
- Each generated landscape also carries its own `conversion_report.json` binding it to the
  SHA-256 of the exact inputs (fuel grid, ERA5 file, FIRMS CSV, slope tif, SRTM DEM mosaic)
  it was built from; the mosaic's own tile-level provenance lives in
  `data/<region>/raw/dem/srtm/<region>_dem_manifest.json` (untracked, regenerable via
  `scripts/fetch_srtm_dem.py`).

A prepared data bundle may be archived on Zenodo with a DOI for citeable, fixed snapshots.
